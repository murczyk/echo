Reproduce the source copies by downloading the pinned archives, checking their hashes, then extracting `package/xlsx.mjs` and `src/docToText.js` with their adjacent license files. To reproduce the exact local diff, run `diff -u package/xlsx.mjs vendor/sheetjs/xlsx-0.20.3.mjs` and `diff -u src/docToText.js vendor/jsdoc/docToText.js` after extraction. The maintained SheetJS invariants are finite iterative traversal, physical-sector bounds, visited-sector rejection, exact DIFAT/FAT chain lengths, and bounded mini-stream concatenation. Any candidate update requires the complete hostile, fidelity, cancellation, deadline, and production-CSP suites in the development contract.
